The HP/LP switch

Both descriptions below are quoted from page 2; picking one here moves the toggle on the pedal too.

Not in the manual

What the switch changes in the circuit is not documented. Page 2 gives the audible result only — HP is “increased distortion throughout the Drive Knob's range, more volume, and a slight increase in the midrange (between 1-2 Khz)”, LP is “incredible accuracy to the original sound of your Guitar and amp…very little coloration!” The only number page 2 puts on the difference is the output gain: “about 20dB of available gain (more in HP mode)”.

Fulltone's sample settings

The two starting points printed on page 2, each with the pickup and amp it assumes. Both give Tone as “set to preference”, so loading one moves Volume, Drive and the toggle, and leaves the Tone knob where you had it.
