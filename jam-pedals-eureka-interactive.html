Eureka!Fuzz · three knobs · three-way toggle

Three knobs and one switch. JAM, on page 1: “We set out to create a circuit that combined the singing, dynamic mid-range of germanium fuzz pedals with the gritty, full-bodied and well-rounded Muff-style fuzz distortion.” The circuit uses “silicon transistors in a proprietary architecture”. The rest of the leaflet is the toggle, the three knobs and the specifications, all below.

What the pedal is set to

Controls, as the manual lists them

The three-way toggle

The manual lists it under CONTROLS (pg. 1) as “Toggle – switch selects between 3 increments of bass boost: — 0 bass boost in middle position — +1 bass boost in ‘!’ position — +2 bass boost in [⚡] position”. Nowhere does the leaflet say how much low end one increment adds, in decibels or otherwise. Pick a position below and the pedal above follows.

What the toggle is for

“The onboard three-way toggle-switch incrementally introduces more low-end, a feature extremely useful for switching between single-coil and humbucker equipped guitars, as well as beefing things up for solos!” (pg. 1)

Which position goes with which guitar is not stated.

The gated-fuzz response

“When set to the [⚡] setting, and sending a strong enough signal its way you can enjoy its gated-fuzz response!” (pg. 1)

✓ Toggle at ⚡Not there yet.
✓ A strong enough input signalThe leaflet gives no threshold. Not in the manual: what arrives at the input depends on your pickups, anything boosting in front of the pedal, and how hard you play — none of which this page can see, so this one stays unticked.

The endorsements on page 2

Page 2 of the leaflet is three players’ endorsements: Mike Miller, James Sedwards and David Torn. Miller is the one who takes up this switch: “With the toggle switch to the right, there’s a kind of compression there, where the attack is smeared out before the gigantic note arrives.”

Where the manual stops

The leaflet does not say which way the tone control brightens, what range it covers, or where the gain knob sits for unity gain. This page does not fill them in either.
