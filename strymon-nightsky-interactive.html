NightSkyTime-Warped Reverberator

Ten knobs and eight buttons, grouped into five labelled sections — TONE, DECAY, VOICE, MOD and MIX — plus eight preset buttons and three footswitches. That much is printed on the enclosure.

Holding a control changes what the other controls do, and none of that is printed on the enclosure. Hold one of the eight buttons and the REVERB knob stops setting the wet output level and starts adjusting a parameter with no knob of its own. Hold the INFINITE footswitch and the eight preset buttons become sequencer steps. Hold INFINITE while the power comes on and five preset buttons and three knobs set global options instead. The panel below relabels itself for each of those; pick a mode, pick a button to hold, and the drawing follows.

Reverb mode

This panel relabels itself; none of the knobs make a sound. What every control does is quoted from the manual and attributed to the printed page it is on. The value beside it is computed here from the drawn knob position. Three of those values are worked out rather than read straight off the knob, and each says so where it appears: the SIZE/PITCH offset in semitones is derived from the pitch range table on printed page 6; the SPEED rate in hertz is interpolated between the two endpoints the manual publishes; and the INTERVAL knob is divided into ten equal segments, one per printed mark plus the two detune settings, because the manual names the marks but not the angles between them. Where the manual gives no numbers at all — LENGTH, LOW CUT, HIGH CUT, SHIMMER, DEPTH, the Live Edit parameters — the value is a clock position and a percentage.

The five sections

Four of the five boxes on the enclosure open with a one-line summary in the manual, quoted below. Page 12 gives MIX no summary line and goes straight to its two knobs.

Hold a button, turn REVERB

Seven of the eight buttons have a second job. Press and hold one and turn the REVERB knob; the exception is QUANTIZE, which is held while you press a preset button instead. Strymon call these Live Edit functions.

SIZE/PITCH, QUANTIZE and the scales

SIZE/PITCH is the knob the sequencer drives and the knob MIDI notes play. Of QUANTIZE, the manual says: Selects the response and range of the SIZE/PITCH knob.

SIZE/PITCH and the pitch range tablepg. 6

Increases the size of the reverb core to create a larger “space” for reflections as the knob is turned clockwise.Strymon

The same page prints a pitch range table. Under For smooth mode it gives three lines: 12 o’clock = middle of range, minimum = 1.25 octaves up and maximum = 1.25 octaves down.Strymon

The three QUANTIZE settingspg. 6

smooth — SIZE/PITCH varies smoothly and continuously over a 2.5 octave pitch range.

half step — SIZE/PITCH is quantized into half-step intervals over a two octave range.

scale — SIZE/PITCH is quantized into selectable scales over a two octave range.Strymon

Turning SIZE/PITCH with audio already in the bufferpg. 6

Strymon's note: When there is audio in the reverb buffer or held by engaging INFINITE, adjusting SIZE/PITCH will change the pitch of the reverberated audio.Strymon

Playing SIZE/PITCH from MIDIpg. 6

Strymon's tip: You can “play” the SIZE/PITCH knob using MIDI notes C-2 to C0. Sending a MIDI note in this range will automatically set the QUANTIZE mode to half step.Strymon

Not the manual — a reading of an apparent misprint

Under For half-step and scale modes, the pitch range table prints minimum = 1 octave up on one line and maximum = 1 octave up on the next. An octave up at each end of the knob leaves no range at all, and the QUANTIZE entry on the same page says half step is quantized into half-step intervals over a two octave range. This page therefore reads the second line as an octave down, which is also the direction smooth mode runs in. The semitone figures in the panel above follow that reading. The manual does not print it.

The DRIVE, SHIMMER and FILTER buttons each choose between two positions in the signal path. Strymon's caption to the diagram: Keep in mind that DRIVE, SHIMMER, and FILTER blocks can be located in only one of the shown locations. SIZE/PITCH and LENGTH feed the pitch regeneration

Smaller SIZE/PITCH settings and higher LENGTH settings will result in more intense pitch regeneration. Strymon, pg. 8

Not the manual — which SHIMMER setting that sentence covers

Strymon print that sentence under the regen description on page 8, and describe input as a shimmer that does not regenerate. On that basis this page treats SIZE/PITCH and LENGTH as changing the intensity of regen shimmer only; the manual never says which of the two settings the sentence belongs to.

Sequence mode

NightSky has a built-in eight step sequencer where each step can have an independent SIZE/PITCH parameter setting for creating rhythmical pitch patterns with the currently loaded reverb settings. Sequences are saved per preset. Strymon, pg. 21

Entering and leaving Sequence modepg. 21

To enter Sequence mode, press and hold the INFINITE (sequence) footswitch until the ON LED turns RED. PRESET BUTTONS now become sequence steps 1 through 8 with an AMBER button indicating that the step is active.Strymon

Holding INFINITE again leaves Sequence mode: Press and hold to toggle in and out of Sequence mode.Strymon, pg. 18

Strymon's note: An unlit PRESET BUTTON will be skipped over and does not indicate a rest.Strymon

Programming a steppg. 22

Press a PRESET BUTTON to enable (AMBER button LED) or disable (button LED OFF) the sequence step. Press and hold a PRESET BUTTON and turn the SIZE/PITCH knob to set the desired pitch for each sequence step. A minimum of two active steps are required for the sequence to run.Strymon

Strymon's tips: It is helpful to have audio in the reverb buffer while you program the sequence. This can be done by playing audio through the pedal or holding the audio using the INFINITE footswitch. and Try setting QUANTIZE to half step or scale for more harmonious sequences.Strymon

Running the sequencepg. 23

Tap a tempo using the FAVORITE (sequence tap) footswitch to run the sequencer at the rate of the tapped tempo. A PRESET BUTTON LED will illuminate RED to indicate the current step. The maximum step length is 2000ms and the sequence will automatically run if a second tap is not entered before the maximum step length.Strymon

A single press of the FAVORITE (sequence tap) footswitch resets the sequencer to run from step 1 if the sequencer is already running. If NightSky is receiving MIDI clock, a single press will run the sequence.Strymon

Stepping the sequence by footpg. 24

Press the ON (sequence step) footswitch to advance the sequence to the next step with each press. A PRESET BUTTON LED will illuminate RED to indicate the current step.Strymon

Strymon's note: If the sequencer is running, pressing the ON (sequence step) footswitch once will stop the sequence and return to the first step.Strymon

Sequence Glide can be adjusted to add portamento to smooth the changes between the sequence steps. Press and hold the SHAPE button and turn the REVERB knob to adjust the amount of Sequence Glide.Strymon, pg. 23

Determines whether new audio received at the inputs will continue to be processed by the reverb while INFINITE is active. Press and hold the TARGET button and turn the REVERB knob. The status will be shown on PRESET BUTTON 1. Green is New audio is processed by the reverb while INFINITE is ON; red is New audio is not processed by the reverb while INFINITE is ON. Strymon, pg. 27

Morph

The Morph function allows NightSky to sweep between the current sound of the pedal and a set of alternate knob settings and back by holding down the ON footswitch. Morph parameter settings are saved per preset. Strymon, pg. 28

Setting up Morph parameterspg. 28

Strymon's six steps, in their words:

  1. Press and hold PRESET BUTTONS 2 & 7 together to enter MORPH setup mode. When PRESET BUTTONS 1-8 all flash GREEN, release the buttons.
  2. Adjust the knobs to the desired MORPH setting.
  3. Press any PRESET BUTTON to exit MORPH setup mode.
  4. Adjust the knobs back to the desired sound.
  5. Press and hold ON footswitch to sweep between the current setting and the Morph setting.
  6. Release ON footswitch to return to the current setting of the pedal.

Morph speedpg. 28

Sets the amount of time it takes to arrive at the MORPH setting. Press and hold the ON footswitch and turn the REVERB knob to adjust the Morph Speed.Strymon

Presets

There are 16 onboard presets that are accessible by using the 8 PRESET BUTTONS on NightSky. They are arranged in 2 banks of 8 presets with different PRESET BUTTON colors indicating the selected bank. Over MIDI there are more: NightSky contains 300 preset locations, numbered sequentially from 0-299. (pg. 36) Strymon, pg. 19

Selecting a presetpg. 19

PRESET BUTTONS 1-8 select the active onboard preset and engage the FAVORITE footswitch if not already engaged. Press the lit PRESET BUTTON to switch between two banks. GREEN LED indicates presets 1-8, AMBER indicates presets 9-16.Strymon

Saving a presetpg. 20

Press and hold the FAVORITE footswitch until the LED flashes BLUE to enter SAVE mode, then release the footswitch. Then press FAVORITE again to save to the currently selected location, or Press one of the eight PRESET BUTTONS to select a new preset location, and then press the FAVORITE footswitch to save to that location. Press the same PRESET BUTTON twice before pressing the FAVORITE footswitch to save to a different preset bank.Strymon

To exit without saving, press the INFINITE footswitch.Strymon

What a preset holdspg. 20

Saving a preset saves all knob, switch, and button settings, as well as all Live Edit functions, expression control settings, morph settings, and the current sequence.Strymon

The onboard presets 1-16 are assigned to MIDI Program Changes 0-15.Strymon

Power Up mode: the global settings

Global parameters and functions can be accessed via a power up procedure. All power up functions persist through power cycles. Press and hold the INFINITE footswitch while connecting power to the pedal. Once the three footswitch LEDs light up, release the footswitch. Press any footswitch to exit power up mode. Strymon, pg. 29

Not the manual — where on the knob

For the SPEED, LENGTH and REVERB knobs the manual gives the options and their LED colours but no knob positions. Two endpoints are documented: Turn the SPEED knob all the way counter-clockwise to set the MIDI channel to Channel 1 and Turn the LENGTH knob all the way counter-clockwise to set the TRS MIDI OUT mode to ON (Strymon, pg. 34). Everywhere else, the panel at the top of this page divides each knob into equal segments in the order the manual lists the options. Strymon do not print that division.

Factory reset

Restore the pedal to factory power up modes and Live Edit Functions. Press and hold the ON footswitch while powering up the pedal. Once the ON LED starts flashing, release the footswitch. Turn LENGTH knob from 0-100% and back two (2) times. The FAVORITE LED will change colors at the extremes of the knob range and blink RED to indicate when the reset is taking place. Strymon list the colours turn by turn: amber, red, amber, then All three footswitch LEDs flash RED, NightSky resets and restarts. Strymon, pg. 40

The EXP jack and external control

The EXP jack runs in one of four modes, chosen in Power Up mode: Turn the REVERB knob to select the mode (see below). The current mode will be displayed on the INFINITE LED. Strymon, pg. 15

Expression pedal setuppg. 32

With the EXP jack in Expression mode and a TRS pedal connected: Press and hold the PRESET BUTTONS 1 & 8 together for one second until all the PRESET BUTTONS are flashing GREEN. Rock the pedal to the heel position, where buttons 1–4 flash green, and Set the knobs the way you would like them to be in the HEEL position. Once a knob is turned, PRESET BUTTONS 1-4 will flash RED. Repeat toe down with buttons 5–8, then Press any PRESET BUTTON to store and exit setup.Strymon

We recommend using an expression pedal with a 25k Ohm minimum potentiometer. Expression pedal settings are saved and recalled as part of the onboard favorite setting and MIDI accessible presets.Strymon, pg. 33

MultiSwitch Pluspg. 34

Enter Power Up mode, then: Turn the SPEED knob all the way counter-clockwise to set the MIDI channel to Channel 1. The ON LED should be GREEN. Turn the LENGTH knob all the way counter-clockwise to set the TRS MIDI OUT mode to ON. The FAVORITE LED should be GREEN. Turn the REVERB knob to the desired MultiSwitch Plus function. Press INFINITE to store, then connect a TRS cable to the EXP jack.Strymon

Press and hold the A footswitch on Multiswitch Plus when connecting the TRS cable to MultiSwitch Plus for Preset mode. Press and hold the C footswitch on MultiSwitch Plus when connecting the TRS cable to MultiSwitch Plus for Preset Scroll mode or MOD/TONE/VOICE Bypass Mode. Strymon's note: If NightSky is in Sequence mode, loading a new preset will stop the sequence and cause the pedal to switch to Reverb mode.Strymon, pg. 35

MIDI inpg. 14

The 5-pin DIN input receives MIDI Program Change (PC): Recall up to 300 presets. MIDI Continuous Controller (CC): Control the individual parameters of NightSky including all knobs, switches, buttons, and Live Edit functions. MIDI Clock: Syncs the sequencer tempo with an external MIDI Clock source.Strymon

Strymon's note: NightSky can respond to MIDI messages received at the MIDI IN, USB port, or the EXP jack.Strymon

The 300 locations are grouped into three MIDI patch banks — presets 0–127, 128–255 and 256–299 — and NightSky always powers up in MIDI Patch Bank 0. Selecting bank 0, patch 127 will put NightSky into Manual mode. In this mode, the pedal will be set to the current knob and button settings. No preset data can be stored at this preset location.Strymon, pg. 36

Two MIDI settings held on preset buttons 1 & 4pg. 26

Respond to MIDI clock — Determines whether NightSky’s sequencer will respond to MIDI Clock. Press and hold PRESET BUTTONS 1 & 4, turn the SPEED knob. ON LED color will indicate the status. MIDI Clock Off = RED LED (default)Strymon

MIDI expression — Allows you to send MIDI CC# 100 as a MIDI expression pedal. Press and hold PRESET BUTTONS 1 & 4, turn the LOW CUT knob. ON LED color will indicate the status.Strymon
