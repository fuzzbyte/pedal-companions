ACS1Amp + Cab Simulator

Three amp models and six cabinet impulse responses — three cabs, each captured with two microphones. Every amp can be put in front of every cab from the front panel, and the left and right channels can each hold a different amp and cab. Four of the six knobs also do a second job while the Bypass switch is held down, and two of them hold a second value for the Boost.

Three amps into six cabs

Eighteen combinations, all reachable from the front panel. The Cab toggle picks the SM57 capture of a cabinet; held with Bypass it picks the Royer 121 capture of the same cabinet. we have laid these out based on the amp we like using them with, but there is no right or wrong. Mix and match however you like!
